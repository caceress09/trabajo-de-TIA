Best Restaurants & Gastronomy

Discover the best culinary spots in Villa General Belgrano. From traditional Central European dishes to artisan craft beers and delicious bakeries.

Classic

Ciervo Rojo

El Ciervo Rojo is located right in the center of Villa General Belgrano, in the Calamuchita Valley, surrounded by beautiful views and distinguished by its Alpine architecture.

"El Ciervo," as many call it, has become a Villa General Belgrano classic since first opening its doors in late 1964.

All Day

Zula

Zula is a well-known restaurant in Villa General Belgrano, located across from the event hall; it offers breakfast, lunch, afternoon snacks, and dinner.

Bakery & Craft Beer

Rissen

Offers a peaceful and welcoming atmosphere with generous portions of delicious cakes and excellent craft beers. They serve a wide variety of cakes and empanadas, highly recommended for their quality and flavor.

German Cuisine

Ottilia

A German-inspired restaurant featuring traditional German and Swiss dishes alongside Argentine cuisine. Enjoy quality craft beer and live music on Saturday nights. Don't forget to try the strudel with cream and the goulash with spätzle!
