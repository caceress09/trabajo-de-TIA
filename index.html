A LITTLE PIECE OF EUROPE IN CORDOBA

Welcome to Villa General Belgrano

Villa General Belgrano is a charming town located in the Calamuchita Valley, surrounded by mountains, forests and beautiful landscapes.

Its Central European influence can be seen in its architecture, food, festivals and local traditions.
